# Meeting Brief

## Purpose

Read the supplied meeting notes and identify the purpose of the discussion. Write a concise brief for a teammate who did not attend. Use only information found in the notes and preserve the meaning of each decision. Do not invent participants, dates, agreements, or commitments.

## Decisions

Separate finalized decisions from ongoing proposals under discussion. Provide the rationale for a decision when the notes include it. Maintain all relevant conditions and disagreements that influence the outcome. If the notes contain conflicting statements, clearly outline the conflict rather than selecting a version lacking supporting evidence.

## Action Items

Extract each action along with its responsible party and deadline as they are stated. Indicate missing owners and deadlines as unspecified. Keep separate tasks independent to allow for individual tracking. Do not convert a suggestion into an assigned task unless the notes explicitly support that interpretation.

## Open Questions

Gather any unresolved questions and determine what information the team still requires. Differentiate between missing data and confirmed negative responses. Maintain a neutral tone and refrain from assuming the participant's intended meaning. Include pertinent dependencies to ensure the next individual understands the rationale for why a question remains unresolved.

## Final Check

Review the brief in light of the original notes prior to returning it. Ensure accuracy by verifying names, dates, numbers, and the status of all decisions. Eliminate any duplicate entries while preserving unique responsibilities. Submit the brief with sections dedicated to the meeting's objective, resolved decisions, action items, and outstanding questions.
