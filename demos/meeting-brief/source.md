# Meeting Brief

## Purpose

Read the supplied meeting notes and identify the purpose of the discussion. Write a concise brief for a teammate who did not attend. Use only information found in the notes and preserve the meaning of each decision. Do not invent participants, dates, agreements, or commitments.

## Decisions

Separate confirmed decisions from proposals that are still being discussed. Explain the reason for a decision when the notes provide it. Preserve conditions and disagreements that affect the outcome. If the notes contain conflicting statements, describe the conflict clearly instead of choosing a version without evidence.

## Action Items

Extract each action together with its owner and deadline when these are stated. Mark missing owners and deadlines as unspecified. Keep independent tasks separate so that a reader can track them individually. Do not turn a suggestion into an assigned task unless the notes explicitly support that interpretation.

## Open Questions

Collect unresolved questions and identify information that the team still needs. Distinguish missing information from a confirmed negative answer. Keep the wording neutral and avoid guessing what a participant intended. Include relevant dependencies so the next person can understand why a question remains open.

## Final Check

Review the brief against the original notes before returning it. Check names, dates, numbers, and the status of every decision. Remove duplicate points while retaining distinct responsibilities. Return the brief with sections for the meeting purpose, confirmed decisions, action items, and open questions.
